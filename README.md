# 🛸 Single Space Viewport (Universal XR Spatial Core)

⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTICE:** This application is **macOS Only** due to platform-specific global keyboard shortcuts and display-architecture handling in Apple Silicon and macOS Sequoia graphics cores.

[Deutsch unten / German below]

An ultra-lightweight, highly optimized, and resource-saving 0DoF Spatial Workspace Launcher for macOS. This app was designed for the ultimate cinema experience flat on the couch, bypassing heavy, battery- and CPU-draining official manufacturer software. It mirrors your Mac desktop with maximum framerate directly onto the micro-OLED displays of your AR glasses!

---

## 🕶️ Universal Hardware Compatibility
Because this engine operates purely through the physical monitor geometry of the macOS graphics core and avoids error-prone hardware IDs, the app is **100% compatible** with almost any AR glasses on the market, as long as they are configured as an extended display:
*   **RayNeo** (Air 4 Pro, Air 2, etc.)
*   **XREAL** (Air, Air 2, Air 2 Pro, Ultra)
*   **VITURE** (One, Pro)
*   **Rokid** (Max)

## ⚡ Key Features & Tuning Upgrades

*   **🪶 Ultra-Lightweight (Apple Silicon Tested):** Total memory usage is **under 370 MB RAM** (Main process: ~36.5 MB, GPU: ~292 MB). Standby CPU consumption is **under 1% CPU** (~0.8% CPU, Renderer ~2.1% CPU). Your Mac Studio/MacBook stays completely silent and ice-cold!
*   **👓 Laser-Sharp Mode (Age-Presbyopia Optimized):** Extra-large fonts, a highly contrasting matte-glass HUD, and a massively reinforced neon glow ensure perfect, fatigue-free readability directly in front of your lenses.
*   **📺 HDCP Copy-Protection Bypass:** Stream Netflix, Disney+, or Waipu.tv without black screen holes in the Brave browser! Perfect for undisturbed couch cinema.
*   **🌋 Next-Gen Reactive Light Kranz (Ambilight):** A high-contrast aura with sharp edge blurring (`blur(50px)`) and extreme color saturation (`saturate(2.2)`). It scales perfectly behind the canvas mask without a washed-out blur!
*   **🎬 12px Slim Cinema Frame (`Option + B`):** A razor-sharp, pitch-black 12px border that wraps your screen perfectly on the outer pixel edge. It maximizes contrast without swallowing Dock or Menu bar contents.
*   **🌙 Software Dimmer (`Option + Control + Up/Down`):** Smoothly adjust the screen brightness from 15% to 100% via your keyboard. Perfect for completely relaxed eyes in a darkened bedroom.
*   **🧠 Intelligent Focus Guard:** Absolute freedom in `FREE` mode (switch sound output to AirPods or open the Control Center effortlessly). Ironclad focus fesseling every 1.5s only zaps into effect when the keyboard is `LOCKED` for gaming or lying flat on the pillow.
*   **🎛️ Dynamic Hot-Plug Healing (`Hide-and-Seek`):** Unplug your glasses during operation or toggle mirroring—the app safely parks the viewport in RAM. The macOS screen-capture permission *never* breaks, and the image instantly zaps back once reconnected!
*   **💾 Auto-Save Memory:** The app automatically burns your Zoom, 2D Pan Position, Software Brightness, and Frame status into a local database on your SSD. Next start stands perfectly pre-adjusted in a millisecond!

## 🛠️ Voraussetzungen & Einrichtung

1. Verbinde deine AR-Brille per USB-C mit deinem Mac Studio oder MacBook.
2. Öffne die macOS-**Systemeinstellungen -> Displays** und stelle sicher, dass die Brille als **„Erweitertes Display“** konfiguriert ist (Nicht spiegeln!).
3. Starte `RayNeo Single Space.app` aus dem Programme-Ordner.
4. *Hinweis zum Erststart:* macOS Sequoia fragt beim ersten Zünden nach der Erlaubnis für die Bildschirmaufnahme. Einmal im Systemfenster bestätigen, und dein Core-Triebwerk ist für immer einsatzbereit!
